## 1. The rule — what moves and what does not

Exactly one quantity changed: **the quantile used to build a published confidence interval.**
`estimateMean` (and therefore `pairedDifferenceEstimate`, and therefore every interval this
repository prints) now uses Student-t at `n − 1` degrees of freedom at every `n`, where it
previously used `z` above `n = 25`.

**Unchanged, everywhere:**

- every **mean** / point estimate / mean difference;
- every **relative effect** (`−29.7 %`, `+12.0 %`, …);
- every **exactly-zero paired-difference count** (`497/500`, `296/300`, `30/30`, …);
- every **`rho`**, trace digest, CRN alignment audit and saturation count;
- every **`requiredReplications`** figure (`needs n ≈ 579`, `n ≈ 3622`) — `verdict.ts`
  `replicationsToResolve` uses an explicit `Z_95` constant that this change does not touch.

**Changed:**

- every **half-width**, **lower bound**, **upper bound** and **noise floor**, multiplied outward by
  `t(n−1, α) / z(α)`;
- consequently, any **verdict** whose interval sat within that multiplier of zero;
- **and, since 2026-07-27, the sequential stopping rule's quantile too.** The previous revision said
  the `n ≤ 25` t / `n > 25` z crossover was "deliberately left intact" in `halfWidthQuantile`. It
  was intact and it was **dead**: nothing injected it, and `validation/harness.ts:176` had already
  been building `productionStoppingRule` out of `estimateMean`. That symbol is now deleted and the
  stopping rule is t-always by decision rather than by accident. See § 7 and `DECISIONS.md` § D7.

### Direction is one-way for a single interval. It is **not** one-way for a Pareto front.

The interval only ever *widens*, so on **one** comparison an `INDISTINGUISHABLE` can never become
`BETTER` or `WORSE` and only the reverse is possible.

The previous revision generalised that to "no claim in this repository gets *stronger*". **That is
false.** `tuning/report/pareto.ts:505 dominanceOf` returns `'dominates'` iff
`better > 0 && worse === 0`, so a widening that turns a `WORSE` axis into `INDISTINGUISHABLE` moves
a pair from `mutually-non-dominated` to **`dominates`** — a strictly stronger claim, which also
drops the rival off the front. `isIndistinguishable` (`pareto.ts:518`) has the same shape, as does
any criterion phrased *"must not be significantly worse"*.

**Checked, not assumed: no currently-published Pareto front is affected.**

- Every front in `tuning/report`'s suites is built from synthetic fixtures at `n ≤ 24`
  (`holdoutRound.test.ts:68` `REPLICATIONS = 24`; `pareto.test.ts`, `build.test.ts` and
  `format.test.ts` are literal fixtures at n = 12). Below n = 26 the published interval was already
  Student-t, so nothing there can move.
- The only front built from real runs is the holdout round on Garden Apartments. Re-run at n = 60
  (§ 4f), each of its 12 `(candidate, role, objective)` verdicts is identical under `z` and under
  `t`: `c-deadband-2` is BETTER on wt95/tuning, BETTER on awt+wt95/holdout, INDISTINGUISHABLE on
  awt/tuning; `c-deadband-5` is INDISTINGUISHABLE on every quotable axis; `energy` is UNQUOTABLE on
  both arms in both roles. Front membership (`front: ["c-deadband-2"]`) is unchanged.

The corrected argument is recorded in `pareto.ts`'s `dominanceOf` docstring so the next person to
change a quantile, a confidence level or `maxInvalidFraction` has to check the fronts rather than
wave it through as conservative.

### 4.0 Method, and how to reproduce it

Every row below comes from re-running the study that produced the number, against the fixed source,
at the study's own seed and replication budget, and reading the **full-precision `MeanEstimate`**
off the result. The old bound is then recomputed as `mean ± z·SE` (or `mean ± t(n−1)·SE` at
`n ≤ 25`, where nothing moved) and both are rendered at the precision the file prints. No arithmetic
is performed on printed text anywhere.

**The reproduction is validated by its own output:** every recomputed *old* bound in this section
reproduces the currently-published text exactly, character for character, on all 154 intervals in
`benchmark/index.ts`. A reproduction that did not would mean the study had drifted, and § 4e says
where that is in fact the case.

## 7. The stopping rule — resolved, not deferred

The previous revision's § 7 described a "one behavioural side effect outside the reporting path" and
left it as a choice for a later owner. It is now decided. `DECISIONS.md` § **D7** carries the
argument; the short form:

- `validation/harness.ts:176` builds `productionStoppingRule` by injecting `estimateMean`, which
  after T2 is Student-t at every `n`. So the production stopping rule silently changed family, and
  the crossover `docs/03-traffic-and-statistics.md` § Part 3 states was preserved in
  `halfWidthQuantile` — **which had no non-test caller at all**, its own docstring's caller list
  ("the runner's injected `HalfWidthEstimator`, and the convergence report's fallback label") being
  false on both counts.
- The two candidate fixes were: inject a crossover estimator so § Part 3's rule is implemented in
  the loop control; or delete the symbol and correct the doc.
- **Chosen: delete.** `acceptableRange` is a half-width target and `ConvergenceReport.status`
  decides `converged` from the *published* half-width, so a loop control with a narrower quantile
  can stop a cell while that cell's own report says `IN PROGRESS`. Two numbers for the same
  quantity on the same cell is finding #14's failure mode one layer down. It is also the direction
  `runner/stopping.ts` warns about: the crossover stops **earlier**, and "one that stops too early
  publishes a number it did not earn".
- `T_DISTRIBUTION_MAX_N` went with it; after the deletion it had no code use.
- **No replication count changed**, in the suite or anywhere else: `productionStoppingRule` was
  already injecting `estimateMean` before this change. What was removed is a dead alternative.

**Hand-back:** `docs/03-traffic-and-statistics.md` § Part 3's four-line rule still writes the
`t` (n ≤ 25) / `z` (n > 25) crossover. It must become `t[n-1]` at every `n`, or say explicitly that
the crossover describes the literature and not this simulator. `docs/` is not T6's.
