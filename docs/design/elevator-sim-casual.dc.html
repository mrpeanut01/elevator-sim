The shaft decision

A fourth car is 34 units and eight nights. Buy it in week two and you hand back two cars for eight days you still have to clear, in exchange for a fortnight of a building that finally works. Buy it in week four and you have paid for something you never get to use.

The alternative is four cheap things that each shave a second. They will out-perform the shaft this month and leave the building exactly as short as it was.
